# Support Heroes | 助戰英雄

Support Heroes is a cooperative feature in the Player Network system that allows network members to share and borrow heroes for dungeon runs.

助戰英雄是玩家網路系統中的合作功能，允許網路成員分享和借用英雄進行地下城探索。

---

## 📖 Overview | 概覽

When you join a Player Network, you can:
- **Set up to 2 of your heroes as support heroes** for network members to use
- **Borrow support heroes** from other network members for your dungeon runs
- **Use node points** from your network to access support heroes

當你加入玩家網路後，你可以：
- **設定最多 2 個英雄作為助戰英雄**供網路成員使用
- **借用其他網路成員的助戰英雄**進行地下城探索
- **使用網路的節點點數**來使用助戰英雄

---

## 🎯 How It Works | 運作方式

### Setting Support Heroes | 設定助戰英雄

1. **Join a Player Network** first (you must be in a network to use this feature)
2. Go to **Inventory → Heroes Tab**
3. Click **"Set Support Heroes"** button
4. Select up to 2 heroes from your inventory
5. Click **"Confirm"** to set them as support heroes

**Important Notes:**
- Only heroes in your **inventory** can be set as support heroes (not heroes already in teams)
- Once set, these heroes will move to the "support" location
- You can still add your own support heroes to your teams
- To return them to inventory, you must unset them as support heroes first

1. 首先**加入玩家網路**（必須在網路中才能使用此功能）
2. 前往**背包 → 英雄標籤**
3. 點擊**「設定助戰英雄」**按鈕
4. 從背包中選擇最多 2 個英雄
5. 點擊**「確認」**將其設為助戰英雄

**重要提示：**
- 只有**背包中**的英雄可以設為助戰英雄（不能是已在隊伍中的英雄）
- 設定後，這些英雄會移動到「助戰」位置
- 你仍然可以將自己的助戰英雄編入隊伍
- 若要將其返回背包，必須先取消設定為助戰英雄

---

### Using Support Heroes | 使用助戰英雄

1. **Form a team** for a dungeon in the Team Formation screen
2. You'll see a **support hero slot** in your team (max 1 support hero per team)
3. Click to browse **available support heroes** from your network members
4. Select a support hero to add to your team
5. **Cost**: Using a support hero in a dungeon run consumes **1 Node Point** from your network

**Node Point Requirements:**
- Each dungeon run with a support hero costs **1 Node Point**
- Node Points are a **shared network resource**
- If your network has **0 Node Points**, you cannot use support heroes
- Network admins can see node point usage in the network funds page

1. 在隊伍編成畫面中**組建隊伍**
2. 你會看到隊伍中有一個**助戰英雄槽位**（每隊最多 1 個助戰英雄）
3. 點擊瀏覽來自網路成員的**可用助戰英雄**
4. 選擇一個助戰英雄加入隊伍
5. **消耗**：在地下城中使用助戰英雄會消耗網路的 **1 點節點點數**

**節點點數要求：**
- 每次帶助戰英雄進入地下城消耗 **1 點節點點數**
- 節點點數是**網路共享資源**
- 如果網路有 **0 點節點點數**，則無法使用助戰英雄
- 網路管理員可以在網路資金頁面查看節點點數使用情況

---

## 💎 Node Points | 節點點數

### What are Node Points? | 什麼是節點點數？

Node Points are a network-wide resource used to access support heroes. They represent the computational power of your network.

節點點數是網路範圍內的資源，用於使用助戰英雄。它們代表網路的計算能力。

### How to Get Node Points | 如何獲得節點點數

Node Points are earned through:
- **Network member contributions** - When members deposit resources to the network
- **Network level ups** - Higher level networks generate more node points
- **Daily maintenance** - Networks gain node points during successful maintenance
- **Completing network objectives** - Special events and achievements

節點點數的獲得方式：
- **網路成員貢獻** - 成員向網路存入資源時
- **網路升級** - 更高等級的網路產生更多節點點數
- **每日維護** - 網路在成功維護期間獲得節點點數
- **完成網路目標** - 特殊活動和成就

### Checking Node Points | 查看節點點數

You can view your network's current Node Points:
- **Team Formation Screen** - Displays current available node points
- **Player Network → Funds Tab** - Detailed node point information
- **Player Network → Overview** - Quick glance at network resources

你可以在以下位置查看網路的當前節點點數：
- **隊伍編成畫面** - 顯示當前可用節點點數
- **玩家網路 → 資金標籤** - 詳細的節點點數信息
- **玩家網路 → 概覽** - 快速查看網路資源

## ⚔️ Combat with Support Heroes | 使用助戰英雄戰鬥

### Rewards & Ownership | 獎勵與所有權

- **Support hero owner**: Earns **2 Decode Points** each time a network member starts a run with their hero — up to **5 rewarded borrows per day**, and the same borrower only counts once per day. Claim them under **Network → Support → Lending Rewards**.
- **Support hero user**: Receives **100% of the run rewards** (Memory, Cache, EXPD, items, etc.)
- **No penalties**: Using support heroes does not reduce rewards

- **助戰英雄所有者**：網路成員每次帶其英雄開場，所有者得 **2 解讀積分**；每日最多 **5 次**計獎，同一位借用者每天只計一次。到「網路 → 助戰 → 借出回饋」領取。
- **助戰英雄使用者**：獲得**100% 的戰鬥獎勵**（Memory、Cache、EXPD、物品等）
- **無懲罰**：使用助戰英雄不會減少獎勵

### Team Composition Rules | 隊伍組成規則

- Maximum **1 support hero per team** (4 own heroes + 1 support hero = 5 total)
- Support heroes **cannot be controlled directly** by the borrower
- Support heroes use their **own equipment and skills** as set by the owner
- Support heroes gain **no experience** from dungeon runs

- 每隊最多 **1 個助戰英雄**（4 個自己的英雄 + 1 個助戰英雄 = 共 5 個）
- 支援英雄**不能由借用者直接控制**
- 支援英雄使用所有者設定的**自己的裝備和技能**
- 支援英雄從地下城中**不獲得經驗**

## 🔧 Managing Your Support Heroes | 管理你的助戰英雄

### Viewing Your Support Heroes | 查看你的助戰英雄

**From Inventory:**
1. Go to **Inventory → Heroes Tab**
2. Click **"View Support Heroes"** button (only visible if you're in a network)
3. This takes you to **Player Network → Support Heroes** tab

**From Player Network:**
1. Open **Player Network** from the phone menu
2. Navigate to **Support Heroes** tab
3. View your current support heroes and network members' support heroes

**從背包：**
1. 前往**背包 → 英雄標籤**
2. 點擊**「查看助戰英雄」**按鈕（只有加入網路才顯示）
3. 這會帶你到**玩家網路 → 助戰英雄**標籤

**從玩家網路：**
1. 從手機選單打開**玩家網路**
2. 導航到**助戰英雄**標籤
3. 查看你當前的助戰英雄和網路成員的助戰英雄

### Changing Support Heroes | 更換助戰英雄

You can change your support heroes at any time:

1. Go to **Player Network → Support Heroes**
2. Your current support heroes are shown at the top
3. Select up to 2 different heroes from your inventory
4. Click **"Set Support Heroes"** to confirm
5. Previous support heroes automatically return to your inventory

你可以隨時更換助戰英雄：

1. 前往**玩家網路 → 助戰英雄**
2. 你當前的助戰英雄顯示在頂部
3. 從背包中選擇最多 2 個不同的英雄
4. 點擊**「設定助戰英雄」**確認
5. 之前的助戰英雄自動返回背包

### Tips for Choosing Support Heroes | 選擇助戰英雄的技巧

**What makes a good support hero?**
- **High level** heroes are more useful to low-level network members
- **Well-equipped** heroes with good armor and modules
- **Versatile heroes** that work in many team compositions (Healers, Tanks)
- **Rare/strong heroes** that not everyone has access to

**什麼是好的助戰英雄？**
- **高等級**英雄對低等級網路成員更有用
- **裝備精良**的英雄，擁有好的盔甲和模組
- **多功能英雄**，適用於多種隊伍組合（治療師、坦克）
- **稀有/強力英雄**，不是每個人都能獲得的

## 🤝 Network Etiquette | 網路禮儀

### Best Practices | 最佳實踐

**As a Support Hero Provider:**
- Keep your support heroes **well-equipped and leveled**
- Choose heroes that **complement common team needs** (Healers, Tanks)
- **Update your support heroes** when you get better ones
- Be proud to help your network members succeed!

**As a Support Hero User:**
- **Use node points wisely** - they're a shared resource
- **Communicate with your network** about difficult dungeons
- **Contribute to your network** by depositing resources
- **Thank your network members** for sharing powerful heroes

**作為助戰英雄提供者：**
- 保持助戰英雄**裝備精良且等級較高**
- 選擇**符合常見隊伍需求**的英雄（治療師、坦克）
- 當你獲得更好的英雄時**更新助戰英雄**
- 為幫助網路成員成功而自豪！

**作為助戰英雄使用者：**
- **明智使用節點點數** - 這是共享資源
- 與網路**溝通**困難的地下城
- 通過存入資源**為網路做貢獻**
- **感謝網路成員**分享強大的英雄

## ❓ FAQ | 常見問題

### Q: Can I use my own support heroes in my teams?
**A:** Yes! You can add your own support heroes to your teams without consuming node points.

### Q: 我可以在自己的隊伍中使用自己的助戰英雄嗎？
**答：** 可以！你可以將自己的助戰英雄加入隊伍，無需消耗節點點數。

---

### Q: What happens if I leave my network?
**A:** Your support heroes automatically return to your inventory, and you lose access to network support heroes.

### Q: 如果我離開網路會怎樣？
**答：** 你的助戰英雄自動返回背包，你將失去使用網路助戰英雄的權限。

---

### Q: Can I see who is using my support heroes?
**A:** Currently, you cannot see real-time usage stats, but this feature may be added in the future.

### Q: 我能看到誰在使用我的助戰英雄嗎？
**答：** 目前無法查看實時使用統計，但此功能可能在未來添加。

---

### Q: Do support heroes die in dungeons?
**A:** No, support heroes cannot permanently die. They function normally in combat but return to their owner afterward.

### Q: 助戰英雄會在地下城中死亡嗎？
**答：** 不會，助戰英雄不會永久死亡。它們在戰鬥中正常運作，但之後會返回給所有者。

---

### Q: Can I use multiple support heroes from the same owner?
**A:** No, you can only use one support hero per team, regardless of who owns them.

### Q: 我可以使用來自同一所有者的多個助戰英雄嗎？
**答：** 不可以，每隊只能使用一個助戰英雄，無論誰擁有它們。

---

### Q: How are node points calculated for my network?
**A:** Node points are gained from member contributions, network level, and successful maintenance. Check the Network Funds page for details.

### Q: 我的網路的節點點數如何計算？
**答：** 節點點數來自成員貢獻、網路等級和成功維護。查看網路資金頁面了解詳情。
