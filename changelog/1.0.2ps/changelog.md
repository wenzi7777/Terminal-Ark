# Terminal Ark Changelog

## APP BUNDLE VERSION v1.0.2 Public Stable
## SERVER BUNDLE VERSION v1.0.2 Public Stable

## Release Date: Nov. 3rd 2026 22:30 JST

### Changelog
- **Neural Network Terminal Calibration** now covers your whole terminal instead of a single dungeon run. Dungeon exploration, AFK, workstation orders, gacha, currency exchanges, the market, gifts, and other reward actions all shift your neural signal, and a calibration is required once it drifts too far.
- Added the **Signal Drift** meter to the home screen and the dungeon status card, so you can see how close you are to the next calibration.
- Calibration is now needed much less often during normal dungeon exploration.
- Calibration rewards are now limited to 3 per day. After that, a successful calibration still resets your signal drift but grants no Swap.
- Staff members now show a role badge on their player profile.
- Fixed an issue where walking back and forth between cleared dungeon rooms could be used to farm calibration rewards.

### Others
- Included various minor fixes and performance optimizations.
